Build Dark Souls PS2 from your own game

This page turns your copy of Dark Souls Remastered (Steam, PC) into a disc image of the PS2 port that plays in PCSX2. The conversion runs on your computer. Your game files never leave it.

Nothing is uploaded

The check in step 1 reads a few game files inside your browser and compares their SHA-256 hashes with the versions the port is tested on. The conversion in step 2 also runs inside this tab and writes the ISO to your disk. This site hosts code only: no game data, and nothing made from it. The ISO you build is made from your copy of the game, so keep it to yourself.

1 Check your game copy

Pick the folder that holds DarkSoulsRemastered.exe. In Steam: right-click the game, then Manage → Browse local files. Only the 15 files the converter depends on are read, and only to hash them.

2 Convert in your browser

The port's own converter runs inside this tab (Python in WebAssembly): it reads your game folder, builds the maps, characters, menus and scripts, and packs them with the latest build of the engine into DarkSoulsPS2.iso. Keep the tab open while it works. It takes a while (tens of minutes; the maps are the slow part) and up to about 4 GB of memory, so use a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox) with other big tabs closed.

How it works

    
The PS2 engine holds no game data: every game value it needs is in the packs, so one engine build from the
    project's CI serves everyone. This page runs the same Python converter the developers use (in Pyodide, with its C
    helpers compiled to WebAssembly) on your files, in your browser, and writes the disc image locally.

    
Prefer to do it by hand?
      
Clone the repository and follow the README's quick start, then make -C ps2/proto iso.
